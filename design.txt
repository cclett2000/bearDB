======JAVAFX-NOTES======
Similar to android; #<method-name> calls method from class specified in 'fx:controller'
    - <Button text="Test" onAction="#onHelloButtonClick"/>
fx:id == variable in comp. class
    - <Label fx:id="welcomeText" text="Label"/>
	
Remember to use 'fx:id'
